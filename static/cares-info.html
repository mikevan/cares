CARES

Community Accounting & Resource Engagement System

CARES is an open-source accounting and governance platform built for community-service nonprofit organizations. It delivers professional-grade financial management without requiring volunteers to be accountants.

In one sentence

Volunteer-safe workflows with audit-ready nonprofit financial reporting.

See editions Why open source?

Using REGALIA? That’s the Knights of Columbus edition of CARES.

Why CARES exists
Most nonprofits are run by volunteers — but most accounting software is designed for businesses. CARES closes that gap with nonprofit-native standards, simple entry paths, and governance-grade outputs.

What CARES provides

  • Nonprofit-native accounting aligned to FASB ASC 958
  • Double-entry with safeguards and traceable audit trails
  • Role-based access for admins, treasurers, project leaders, and members
  • Projects & programs tracking for budgets and activity-level reporting
  • Board-ready statements (financial position, activities, cash flows, functional expenses)
  • Import/export to support mail merges, backups, and external analysis

Built for stewardship

CARES is more than bookkeeping. It’s infrastructure for organizations entrusted with donor funds, volunteer time, and public trust.

  • Reduce administrative burden so leaders can focus on service
  • Increase transparency with consistent, reportable data
  • Support audits, grants, and Form 990 preparation with structured outputs
  • Make it easier to transition between volunteer treasurers year to year

REGALIA A CARES Edition

REGALIA — for Knights of Columbus chapters

REGALIA is the Knights of Columbus edition of CARES. It applies Knights-friendly defaults and branding while maintaining the same nonprofit accounting rigor and reporting capabilities.

  • Knights color system and council-friendly terminology
  • Member and project structures aligned to chapter operations
  • Designed for volunteer leadership transitions
  • Ready for multi-chapter consolidation (future roadmap)

Future editions (examples)

CARES is designed to support multiple organizations without forcing forks. Future editions can tailor terminology, branding, and workflow defaults.

  • Parish ministries
  • Civic & fraternal organizations
  • Local service nonprofits
  • Youth & community programs
  • State/district rollups

Open source & transparency

CARES is released under the MIT License. This supports nonprofits with:

  • No licensing fees for nonprofit use
  • Transparent handling of financial data
  • Freedom to self-host or use managed hosting
  • A path for community improvements and review

CARES vs. REGALIA

CARES is the platform. REGALIA is the Knights edition.

  • If you are using REGALIA, you are using CARES.
  • CARES provides the accounting engine, reports, and security.
  • REGALIA provides Knights-specific defaults, terminology, and branding.
