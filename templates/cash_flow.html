This statement uses the direct method, showing actual cash receipts and payments:

• Investing Activities - Cash from purchasing or selling non-cash assets
• Financing Activities - Cash from borrowings, repayments, or net asset transactions
• Beginning + Net Change = Ending Cash - Reconciles to your cash accounts
