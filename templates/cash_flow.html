Understanding the Statement of Cash Flows

This statement uses the direct method, showing actual cash receipts and payments:
• Cash Receipts - All revenue received, grouped by project and account
• Cash Payments - All expenses paid, grouped by project and account
• Net Cash from Operating Activities - The difference between receipts and payments
• Beginning + Net Change = Ending Cash - Reconciles to your cash accounts
