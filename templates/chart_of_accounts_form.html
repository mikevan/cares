Account Numbering Guide
Standard Account Number Ranges:
  • 1000-1999: Assets
    • 1000-1099: Cash
    • 1100-1299: Receivables
    • 1500-1599: Fixed Assets
  • 2000-2999: Liabilities
    • 2000-2099: Current Liabilities
    • 2500-2599: Long-term Debt
  • 3000-3999: Net Assets
    • 3100: Unrestricted
    • 3200-3299: Restricted
  • 4000-4999: Revenue
    • 4000-4099: Contributions
    • 4100-4199: Dues
    • 4200-4299: Events
  • 5000-5999: Expenses
    • 5000-5099: Personnel
    • 5200-5299: Occupancy
    • 5300-5399: Supplies
Tips
  • Use consistent numbering within ranges
  • Leave gaps for future accounts (e.g., 1010, 1020, 1030)
  • Parent accounts help organize reports
  • Subtypes group related accounts in financial statements
  • Normal Balance should match account type
