Understanding Functional Expenses

Program Services:

Direct costs of mission-related activities and programs that fulfill the organization's purpose.

Management & General:

Administrative overhead including accounting, legal, board governance, and general operations.

Fundraising:

Costs of soliciting contributions, grants, sponsorships, and other fundraising activities.

Note: This simplified allocation distributes personnel costs (70% program, 20% admin, 10% fundraising). For more precise reporting, track functional allocation at the transaction level.
