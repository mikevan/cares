How to Import Members
  1. Download the template: Click the button below to get a CSV template with the correct format.
  2. Fill in your data: Open the template in Excel or Google Sheets and add your members.
  3. Save as CSV: Make sure to save the file as CSV format (not Excel format).
  4. Upload: Use the upload form to import your members.
Required Fields:
  • name: Member's full name (required)
  • join_date: Format: YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 2024-01-15)
  • active: true/false (default: true)
Important:
  • Members with duplicate emails will be skipped
  • All imported members belong to your current chapter
  • Invalid rows will be reported with specific errors
