About These Reports

All financial reports are generated in compliance with FASB ASC 958 standards for not-for-profit organizations.

  • Balance Sheet: Shows your organization's financial position at a specific point in time
  • Income Statement: Shows revenue and expenses for a period (typically one year)
  • Cash Flow Statement: Shows how cash moved in and out of the organization
  • Functional Expenses: Required FASB report showing expenses by both nature and function (program, administrative, fundraising)

These reports are suitable for grant applications, board meetings, tax reporting, and Form 990 preparation.
