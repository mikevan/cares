Understanding This Entry

Double-Entry Accounting:

Every transaction has two sides:

  • Debit - Money coming in or expenses increasing
  • Credit - Money going out or revenue increasing

Total debits must always equal total credits to maintain balance.

Account Types:

  • Assets: What you own (cash, equipment)
  • Liabilities: What you owe (loans, payables)
  • Net Assets: Your organization's equity
  • Revenue: Money coming in (dues, donations)
  • Expenses: Money going out (rent, supplies)
